## 简介（中文）

把 AI Passport 变成随身的六爻起卦盘。开机即入卦盘：先选所问之事（事业、财运、感情……），填上起卦当天的日期，再按住按键蓄力、松手掷出三枚铜钱 —— 掷六次成卦，铜钱落定的那声脆响里，卦就起了。铜钱分背面与字面，数几个背面就是这一爻；蓄力的轻重也参与其中，所以同一个动作摇两次，卦不会一样。

解卦分页翻看：本卦说眼下，变卦说趋势；爻辞是老古文，下面紧跟一句白话，看不懂「征凶贞厉」也不耽误明白大意；最后一页给出针对所问之事的建议。动爻可能是零个、也可能六个，该读哪条爻辞由变占法决定 —— 那一页会直接写明白。懂行的可以翻到卦盘页细看纳甲、六亲、世应，不讲这些的人看断卦页就够。

摇卦的铜钱声可以调轻、也可以调到静音，档位会记住；搁着不动一会儿，屏幕会自己暗下来、浮现一圈八卦，夜深人静也能安心起一卦。全程离线，不联网、不上传，问什么都不出这台设备。

## Description (English)

A pocket I Ching (Liu Yao) divination set for AI Passport. It opens straight into the reading: pick what you're asking about — work, money, love — set the date, then hold the button to charge and release to toss three coins. Six tosses build your hexagram, and because the force of your press feeds the randomness, no two casts come out the same.

Page through the reading: the primary hexagram shows where things stand, the changing hexagram shows where they're heading, and each classical line is followed by a plain-language explanation, so you don't need to read classical Chinese to get the gist. The number of changing lines decides which text to read — the final page says so outright. That page also offers advice on the matter you asked about.

The coin sound can be lowered or muted and your setting is remembered, and the screen quietly dims into a ring of trigrams when left alone. Everything runs offline — no network, no uploads, nothing leaves the device.
