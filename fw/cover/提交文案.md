## 简介（中文）

把 AI Passport 变成随身的六爻起卦盘。开机即入卦盘：先选所问之事（事业、财运、感情……），填上起卦当天的日期，再按住按键蓄力、松手摇出六枚铜钱 —— 铜钱落定的那声脆响里，卦就起了。

解卦分页翻看：本卦说眼下，变卦说趋势；爻辞是老古文，下面紧跟一句白话，看不懂「征凶贞厉」也不耽误明白大意；最后一页给出针对所问之事的建议。懂行的可以翻到卦盘页细看纳甲、六亲、世应，不讲这些的人看断卦页就够。

摇卦的铜钱声可以调轻、也可以调到静音，档位会记住，夜深人静也能安心起一卦。全程离线，不联网、不上传，问什么都不出这台设备。

## Description (English)

A pocket I Ching (Liu Yao) divination set for AI Passport. It opens straight into the reading: pick what you're asking about — work, money, love — set the date, then hold the button to charge and release to cast six coins. As they land with a soft clink, your hexagram appears.

Page through the reading: the primary hexagram shows where things stand, the changing hexagram shows where they're heading, and each classical line is followed by a plain-language explanation, so you don't need to read classical Chinese to get the gist. The last page offers advice on the matter you asked about.

The coin sound can be lowered or muted and your setting is remembered, so you can cast quietly late at night. Everything runs offline — no network, no uploads, nothing leaves the device.
